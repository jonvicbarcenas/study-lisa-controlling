® Successful Implementation of a disciplinary action:
• Promptness: Action is taken immediately after the
violation
o Example: Nurse manager addresses a staff nurse
who repeatedly arrives late on the same shift, not
weeks later.
• Fairness: Discipline matches severity of the offense.
o Example: Minor documentation errors =
coaching, not suspension.
• Impartiality: Rules applied consistently to all staff,
without favoritism
o Example: Both senior and new nurses receive the
same consequence for medication errors.
• Non-punitiveness: Aim is correction, not punishment
o Example: Nurse manager provides retraining on
IV insertion instead of only issuing a warning for a
failed attempt.
• Advance warning: Staff are informed of rules and
possible consequences beforehand
o Example: Hospital handbook and orientation
clearly state attendance policies.
• Follow-through: Management enforces rules
consistently and doesn’t ignore violations
o Example: Continuous tardiness leads to
progressive discipline (verbal → written → formal
action).
